<h2 id="why-dmops-core">Why dmops-core</h2>

<div class="landing-grid">
  <div class="landing-card">
    <h3>Beside the EDC, never inside it</h3>
    <p>
      No clinical data, no query workflow, no document content. If a fact
      lives in the EDC or eTMF, an adapter reads it; if it lives nowhere
      else, dmops-core owns and audits it. Dual entry is the failure mode
      that kills status portals
      (<a href="/dmops-core/reference/decisions/0001-pmo-beside-the-edc/">ADR-0001</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="landing-card">
    <h3>Governed milestones, honest slip</h3>
    <p>
      One 38-code taxonomy across every study, four dates per milestone, and
      computed slip that survives re-planning: moving a plan is an
      append-only governance action with a required reason
      (<a href="/dmops-core/reference/decisions/0009-append-only-rebaseline-governance/">ADR-0009</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="landing-card">
    <h3>Metrics are code, not dashboards</h3>
    <p>
      Every KPI is one versioned written definition bound to one tested
      compute function, qualified against hand-computed fixtures, and
      snapshotted immutably with extract provenance
      (<a href="/dmops-core/reference/decisions/0004-metrics-are-versioned-code/">ADR-0004</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="landing-card">
    <h3>Unavailable beats approximated</h3>
    <p>
      Adapters declare per-field capabilities; a metric whose source cannot
      supply a required field is skipped and says so on the board. No number
      appears that nobody can defend
      (<a href="/dmops-core/reference/decisions/0005-adapter-capability-contract/">ADR-0005</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="landing-card">
    <h3>Audit below the application</h3>
    <p>
      A hash-chained audit trail written by database triggers, verified end
      to end by <code>/health</code>, and an API role that cannot fabricate
      or edit audit events — controls that hold even for ad-hoc psql
      (<a href="/dmops-core/reference/decisions/0003-db-enforced-audit/">ADR-0003</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="landing-card">
    <h3>Displays regulated records, holds none</h3>
    <p>
      Deliverable and UAT evidence stay in the eTMF; dmops-core stores
      status plus a link, with no signature columns anywhere in the schema.
      Scope discipline keeps the validation burden proportionate
      (<a href="/dmops-core/reference/decisions/0006-display-only-regulated-records/">ADR-0006</a>).
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
